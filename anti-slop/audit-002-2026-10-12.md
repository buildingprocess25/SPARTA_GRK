## Finding 1 — Modal close button is undersized, used by every modal in the app (HIGH)

**Rule:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness), tap target minimum 44x44px — Hard Gate.

**Where:** `src/components/ui/BaseModal.jsx:160`, the shared dialog close button: `className="flex size-9 shrink-0 items-center justify-center ..."` — 36x36px, 8px under the minimum.

**Why it matters more than a normal finding:** `BaseModal` is the shared modal shell used by 7 components across the app (confirmed by grep), including `Scope1InputModal`, `Scope2InputModal`, `WaterRecycleInputModal`, and others. A single fix here corrects the close button on every modal in the dashboard at once. Compare to `Header.jsx:186` and `Sidebar.jsx:139`, which both already use `size-11` (44px) for their own close buttons — this is the one inconsistent spot.

**Fix:** change `size-9` to `size-11` on the close button.

## Finding 3 — `grid-cols-3` with no mobile reflow in two input modals (LOW, unconfirmed)

**Rule:** R-03, columns that don't collapse.

**Where:** `src/components/scope1/Scope1InputModal.jsx:1040` and `src/components/scope2/Scope2InputModal.jsx:835` — both render a 3-column stat grid (Excel import preview: "Total Baris / Valid / Error") with no `sm:`/responsive variant, inside a modal.

**Assessment:** every other multi-column grid found in the codebase (KPI card rows, summary grids) consistently uses the established `grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-4`-style reflow pattern — these two are the only exceptions found. The content in each cell is short (one label word, one number), so it may hold up fine even at 3-across on a narrow modal; the text is small (`text-[10px]`/`text-sm`) and would wrap rather than overflow. I could not confirm actual pixel overflow without rendering the app at a phone width, so this is flagged as unconfirmed rather than a certain violation.

**Fix if approved:** change to `grid-cols-3` only above a breakpoint (e.g. `grid-cols-1 xs:grid-cols-3` or simply drop to 2 columns), matching the pattern used everywhere else in the app. Recommend verifying in a real phone-width viewport before or after the change.
