## Note

This is a small, internal operational dashboard with mostly functional/technical UI copy (labels, methodology notes, form instructions, error messages), not marketing or narrative prose. Several of this skill's patterns (significance inflation, aphorism formulas, staccato drama, weasel attributions, speculative gap-filling) are shaped for longer-form persuasive or narrative writing and have little surface area to appear in this kind of app in the first place. Their absence here is expected, not a thin check.
